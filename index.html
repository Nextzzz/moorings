A stone pub at the gates of Lough Key Marina. Navy walls, a brick hearth, a conservatory full of evening light — and a kitchen that cooks like it has something to prove.
01 — The bar

Pints under Tiffany glass

Guinness, Smithwick's and seven taps on an oak counter. Pool and darts through the back. Live music every Friday night.

02 — The hearth

Dinner by the stove

Brick chimney, logs stacked and lit from October. The room families book for Sunday lunch and keep for the evening.

03 — The conservatory

Long table, low sun

Seats sixteen along the stone wall. Birthdays, christenings, the 27-strong Easter crowd — ring Roger and it's yours.
